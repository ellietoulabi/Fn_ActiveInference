mappo_sample_efficiency_curve.png -- what it shows and how to read it
========================================================================

THE QUESTION THIS PLOT ANSWERS
How much training does MAPPO need before it matches each Active
Inference (AIF) paradigm, which needs zero training at all?

THE THREE HORIZONTAL LINES (Independent / Individually Collective /
Fully Collective)
Each is one AIF paradigm's average soups delivered in a single ~1500-
step Overcooked episode, averaged over 30 independent seeds. AIF does
not train via gradient descent -- it acts by inference from a fixed,
hand-specified model from the very first step of the very first
episode, so there is no 'number of training steps' for it. The line
is drawn horizontally only so its one number can be compared against
MAPPO's curve at every x-position -- it is the same value everywhere,
not a function of the x-axis.

THE SHADED BAND AROUND EACH LINE
95% confidence interval computed from the spread across those 30
seeds -- i.e. how much that paradigm's average would plausibly shift
if you re-ran with a different batch of 30 seeds. A narrow band means
the seeds mostly agree; a wide band means they don't. Where two
paradigms' bands overlap a lot, their means are not reliably
distinguishable from this sample size, even if one number is nominally
higher.

  Independent                mean=3.33  95% CI=+/-0.78  (n=30 seeds)
  Individually Collective    mean=5.17  95% CI=+/-0.84  (n=30 seeds)
  Fully Collective           mean=4.73  95% CI=+/-1.03  (n=30 seeds)

THE PURPLE MAPPO CURVE AND ITS BAND
Each point is the mean soups-delivered over 30 independently-trained
MAPPO policies (different training seeds), evaluated at that many
cumulative training steps. Its shaded band is also a 95% CI across
those 30 training seeds, computed the same way as the AIF bands so the
two are visually comparable.

WHY THE CURVE IS JAGGED, NOT SMOOTH
Each checkpoint is scored on only 1 held-out evaluation episode
per training seed -- not averaged over many eval runs. So every point is
a genuinely noisy single (or few-episode) estimate, not a smoothed
learning curve. This is also why the band widens rather than narrows at
higher budgets: later checkpoints happen to have higher-variance
single-episode outcomes across training seeds, not more uncertainty in
a statistical-estimation sense.

THE LEFTMOST POINT ('0' on the x-axis)
This is the untrained / randomly-initialized policy, evaluated before
any training step. The x-axis uses a symlog scale specifically so this
point can sit at its true value of exactly 0, rather than being faked
onto a nonzero position the way a plain log-scale axis would require.

CROSSOVER POINTS (first plotted budget where MAPPO's mean reaches a
given AIF paradigm's zero-shot mean -- not interpolated, so the true
crossover may be anywhere between this budget and the previous one)
  Independent                reached by 250,000 training steps
  Individually Collective    reached by 400,000 training steps
  Fully Collective           reached by 300,000 training steps
